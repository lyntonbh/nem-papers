Deep learning for NEM spot prices: the papers, and every idea inside them, explained

A directory of every Australian National Electricity Market (NEM) study cited in the related-works section of Learning the Grid (Sinclair, Shepley & Hajati, Applied Sciences 2026), plus that paper itself. Parts 1 to 4 teach the ideas from the ground up, assuming only a basic feel for time series and neural networks. Part 5 is the papers, with every technical term linked back to its explanation.

DirectoryTimeline: seven NEM papers, 2023 to 2026

The thread running through them: everyone starts from raw prices, finds them too noisy, and either (a) decomposes the series before a neural network, (b) adds outside information (weather, market notices, AEMO's own forecasts), or (c) changes the network from recurrent to attention-based. Sinclair 2026 is the first to do (b) and (c) together and to benchmark against AEMO's pre-dispatch forecast.

March 2023 · Electric Power Systems Research
Tan, Shen, Yu & Lu · ICEEMDAN-CNN+SSDAE · NSW day-ahead price
Split the price series into frequency components, forecast each with a CNN feeding a stacked sparse denoising autoencoder, add them back up.
October 2023 · Energy and AI
Ghimire, Nguyen-Huy, Al-Musaylh, Deo, Casillas-Pérez & Salcedo-Sanz · TNET · Queensland substation demand
First NEM-area transformer, but for daily electricity demand, not price. Beat LSTM, BiLSTM, GRU, CNN and DNN at four Energex substations.
March 2024 · IEEE Trans. Energy Markets, Policy & Regulation
Li, Wang, Zhang & Wang · TempDRL · battery bidding in spot + six FCAS markets
Not a price forecaster: a transformer reads the last 160 minutes of seven market prices and a reinforcement-learning agent bids. Tested on all five NEM regions.
July 2024 (online) · Applied Energy
Ghimire, Deo, Casillas-Pérez, Sharma, Salcedo-Sanz, Barua & Acharya · MoDWT-CRVFL · NSW half-hourly price
Wavelet-decompose the price, CNN extracts features, a random-weight one-shot network (RVFL) does the regression, Bayesian optimisation picks the settings.
November 2024 · IECON (IEEE)
Liu, Cai, Dalzell & Mills · fine-tuned LLM + CNN-LSTM · extreme prices in SA and NSW
A large language model reads AEMO market notices and turns them into an event time series that a wavelet CNN-LSTM uses alongside price history.
December 2024 · ICSCGE (IEEE)
Malyala, Thattai, Malik & Ravishankar · GPI-DSSM · RRP from price and demand only
Argues that state-wide weather is noise. A patch-based Informer transformer plus a GRU state-space memory forecast the regional reference price from price and aggregate demand alone.
December 2025 (Applied Sciences vol. 16, 2026)
Sinclair, Shepley & Hajati · encoder-decoder / decoder-only / encoder-only transformers · NSW, QLD, VIC, 2 to 16 h ahead
Plain transformers with AEMO pre-dispatch and weather forecasts as future inputs. Decoder-only wins in NSW and QLD; AEMO's own forecast has 2 to 3 times the error at 8 h and beyond.

Metrics are not comparable across rows. Tan and Ghimire 2024 report errors on normalised or smoothed series and on short or single-year windows; Sinclair reports a normalised MAPE (mean absolute error divided by the mean price) over a 34-month period that contains the 2024 to 2025 spike seasons. Within each paper, though, the comparison against its own baselines is fair. Part 1 explains each metric.
